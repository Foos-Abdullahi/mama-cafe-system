# MaMa Café --- Brand & Design System

## Brand direction

Warm, premium, simple and organized. The interface should feel like MaMa
Café rather than a generic enterprise application.

## Visual language

### Primary colors

-   Espresso Brown --- `#432717`
-   Dark Coffee --- `#2F1B10`
-   Cream --- `#F8F4ED`
-   Warm Beige --- `#F1E1CA`
-   Caramel Gold --- `#9A6323`
-   Soft Brown --- `#D6BEA0`
-   Success Green --- `#4D7A4A`
-   Danger Red --- `#B84A3A`

### Typography

-   Display: elegant serif for brand headings.
-   UI: clean sans-serif for dashboards and forms.
-   Numbers: strong, highly readable weight for totals, sales and
    payroll.

## UI principles

### 1. POS first

The Operations Portal should behave like a familiar POS: - Product
categories at the top. - Product cards in the center. - Current order on
the right. - Large total. - Large payment actions. - Complete Sale
button always visible.

### 2. Dashboard first

Admin and manager screens should surface: - Total sales - Total orders -
Collected - Outstanding - Waitress performance - Top products

### 3. Status clarity

Use consistent status labels: - PAID - PARTIAL - UNPAID - COMPLETED -
CANCELLED - REFUNDED

### 4. Touch-friendly controls

Buttons should be large enough for tablet POS use.

### 5. Minimal data entry

Prefer selection, search and automatic calculations over manual typing.
